Our Story

A small shop built around a good cup.

Since 2024

Why we opened

coffee.piyo began with a simple frustration: it was hard to find a proper cup of coffee in the city that didn’t come with loud music and a rushed counter. We wanted a place where the coffee was taken seriously and the room was calm enough to talk in.

We opened our doors in 2024 with one espresso machine, a short menu and a handful of tables. Most of those first customers still come in, and many of them know our baristas by name.

— The founder, coffee.piyo

How we work

What we believe

Freshness first

Beans are roasted in small batches and used within a few weeks. We’d rather throw a batch away than serve a tired shot.

Fair to farmers

We work with small suppliers and pay prices that let them keep growing good coffee year after year.

Room for everyone

Students, freelancers, families, first dates. If you’re buying one coffee and staying three hours, that’s fine by us.

The space

Brick, wood and plenty of plants

The cafe is designed to feel lived in. Long communal tables for groups, quiet corners for work and a counter where you can watch your drink being made.
