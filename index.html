What we care about

Three things we don’t compromise on

Beans we trust

We buy from small farms and roast in small lots, so what lands in your cup is never more than a couple of weeks off the roaster.

Hands that practise

Our baristas train for weeks before they take an order. Milk is steamed to texture, not to a timer, and every shot is tasted.

A room to stay in

Warm light, brick walls, proper chairs and Wi-Fi that works. Bring a laptop, a book or a friend you haven’t seen in a while.

House favourite

The Piyo Latte

A double shot of our house blend, silky steamed milk and a light touch of caramel. Not too sweet, not too strong. It’s the drink most of our regulars start with.
